# Source: Notes

The owner's own notes. Cheapest source, often the best.

## Where

- `cycles/<cycle>/<slug>/notes.md`: anything dropped in during the cycle.
- `people/<slug>.md`, section "Things I want to remember".
- `voice/samples/`: past reviews of the same person, for history and for promises made last time.

## What to do

Read them whole. Pull out every dated or datable event. Where a note references a PR, ticket or thread, find the link with the other sources and attach it. Where a note is an opinion ("she is great at unblocking people"), keep it aside as an interview prompt rather than evidence: "You noted she is good at unblocking people. When did that happen?"

Past reviews matter for one thing above all: what development feedback was given last time. The interview should ask whether it landed.
